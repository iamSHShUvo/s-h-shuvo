I am an emerging medical physicist with a BSc in Medical Physics & Biomedical Engineering from Gono University, Dhaka (defended February 2026). My work sits at the intersection of computational oncology, radiation physics, and artificial intelligence — applying radiomics and dosiomics pipelines to predict treatment toxicity and evaluate AI model generalizability across diverse populations.

My BSc thesis, "Cross-Population Generalizability of Lung Cancer Radiomics and Dosiomics: Implications for AI Deployment in a South Asian Cohort," is the first forensic audit of radiomic and dosiomic domain shift between a Western public dataset and a multi-centre South Asian cohort. The analysis of 597 patients (TCIA Netherlands + Square Hospital + Ahsania Mission, Dhaka) revealed that Western-trained AI models carry a profound scanner-specific fingerprint that renders them unreliable in the Bangladeshi clinical context. The full codebase is openly published under MIT License.

Beyond the thesis, I have demonstrated research breadth through dosimetric comparative studies (VMAT vs. DIBH-3DCRT for breast cancer), multi-detector IMRT QA frameworks for head & neck cancer, and policy-level analysis of medical physics infrastructure in Bangladesh — all presented at international conferences. I combine rigorous clinical training from Diponegoro University, Rayos Contra Cancer, and RAD-AID with hands-on expertise in Python, MATLAB, 3D Slicer, and radiation treatment planning workflows.

03 — Research

Research Experience

Lead Researcher · 2025–2026 · Clinical Radiomics / AI

Cross-Population Generalizability of Lung Cancer Radiomics & Dosiomics

Spearheaded a multi-centre analytical study using clinical imaging data from two major oncology centres in Dhaka (597 patients total). Engineered AI-driven computational models to analyse domain shifts between a Western public dataset (NSCLC-Radiomics, TCIA) and a novel South Asian cohort — quantifying cross-population generalizability and addressing critical underrepresentation of South Asian populations in radiomics/dosiomics validation pipelines. Developed custom forensic DICOM pipeline recovering 92.5% of legacy cases.

Stack: Jupyter · Python · PyRadiomics · SHAP · PyTorch · 3D Slicer

Dosimetry Research · Peer-Reviewed Abstract

Enhanced Multi-Detector IMRT QA Protocol for Head & Neck Cancer

Developed a comprehensive multi-detector QA framework integrating ionisation chamber array, radiochromic film, and EPID with a risk-based assessment model. Evaluated gamma criteria (3%/3mm, 2%/2mm, 1%/1mm) across 30 H&N IMRT plans. Correlated outcomes with plan complexity metrics (MCS, leaf travel distance) to establish site-specific action levels and optimise clinical workflow.

IUPESM World Congress 2025 — Sydney (Paper ID: 168) · ICMPROI 2025, Dhaka
Dosimetric Comparison Study

Free Breathing VMAT vs. DIBH 3DCRT for Left-Side Breast Cancer

Dosimetric study of 20 patients (10 DIBH-3DCRT; 10 FB-VMAT). Analysed PTV coverage, OAR dose constraints, and DVH metrics. DIBH-3DCRT demonstrated superior cardiac and pulmonary sparing; elevated contralateral lung dose in FB-VMAT identified as a key clinical concern. RGS infrared marker system used for respiratory monitoring.

Research Fair 2024 — Bose Centre, University of Dhaka
